The story behind Working Artist

It started with the books.

When designer and illustrator Maria Nogueira Nössing began making children's books in 2023, she quickly realised how much there was to keep track of: submission deadlines, award dates, press coverage and exhibition clippings, as well as the details behind each book, from contracts and publishing rights to royalty percentages.

Unable to find one tool that covered all her needs, she decided to build one. Working Artist grew from that idea into a tool designed to support artists across disciplines.
