Inside Working Artist

1Works

The details behind every project, from contracts to press coverage.

Every detail of each piece. For books, the contract too: advance, royalty, print run and foreign rights.

2Submissions

All your open calls and deadlines in one place.

Open calls, grants, residencies and publisher proposals, from draft to won. Sent ones stop counting down.

3Events

Exhibitions, book fairs, festivals and more.

Dates that span days, the works you showed, and goals for the ones still ahead.

4Career

A look back at what you’ve done and where your work has taken you.

A timeline, a map and your press and awards, built from what you already entered.

5Manifest

A place to collect ideas and think about what you’d like to do next.

Pictures in the middle, with your quotes, goals and references around them, connected. One board per project, or one per year.

6Contacts

Everyone you work with, in one place.

Galleries, editors, curators and collectors, with their addresses and notes. Gather them into lists, like everyone who gets a card at Christmas, and tick them off as you go.

The story behind Working Artist

From making books to building a tool.

When designer and illustrator Maria Nogueira Nössing began making children's books in 2023, she quickly realised how much there was to keep track of: submission deadlines, award dates, press coverage and exhibition clippings, as well as the details behind each book, from contracts and publishing rights to royalty percentages.

Unable to find one tool that covered all her needs, she decided to build one. Working Artist grew from that idea into a tool designed to support artists across disciplines.
